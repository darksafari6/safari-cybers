## Containment and recovery: decisions under uncertainty

The first checkout comparison points to the rollout, but some customers are still buying successfully. You have authority to pause expansion while the release owner investigates. You do not yet know whether a rollback is safe for orders written during the rollout.

### Decide what is reversible

Pausing limits exposure without claiming to know the root cause. A rollback may restore behavior, but verify data compatibility before reversing the application. If changes are urgent, record what was done, when, and by whom. Preserve evidence where feasible and avoid collecting personal data that the investigation does not need.

### Checkpoint

One healthy checkout after containment is not recovery. Define a time window where error rates stay near the baseline and check the affected region, traffic cohort, and user workflow. Once service is stable, write the follow-up action as a change a named owner can make and verify.
