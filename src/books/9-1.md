## Capstone: an incident with two plausible causes

You are the lead for a fictional appointment service. At 10:00 UTC, the team rolls out a new sign-in release to 20% of instances. At 10:08, another team updates an identity service configuration. At 10:10, sign-in complaints rise. The front page is still healthy. Your job is to build an incident record useful to both responders and stakeholders.

### Evidence packet

```text
10:00  sign-in release deployed to cohort A; cohort B unchanged
10:08  identity configuration changed for Region East
10:10  sign-in failures reported; health page still green
10:13  East A: 18/100 fail; East B: 32/200 fail
10:13  West A:  1/100 fails; West B:  2/200 fail
```

### Worked comparison

East A and East B have failure rates of 18% and 16%; both exceed the West rates of 1%. The regional split explains the observed pattern better than the application cohort alone. It does not establish whether the configuration change or another East-specific factor caused the failure. The next check should narrow which boundary observed the errors and whether requests in East had the same identity routing as before the change.

### Deliverable

Write a one-page timeline. Mark each statement as an observation, an inference, or an open question. Give at least two plausible explanations and identify one next check that would change your confidence in each. Use UTC, and report both percentages and sample sizes so another responder can interpret the rates.
